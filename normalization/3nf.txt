THIRD NORMAL FORM (3NF)

A table is in Third Normal Form when:

1. It is already in 2NF.
2. It has no transitive dependency.

A transitive dependency occurs when a non-key attribute depends on another non-key attribute.

Example:

Student_ID | Student_Name | Department_ID | Department_Name
-----------|--------------|---------------|----------------
1          | Rahim        | 10            | CSE
2          | Karim        | 20            | EEE

Student_ID determines Department_ID.

Department_ID determines Department_Name.

Therefore:

Student_ID -> Department_ID -> Department_Name

This creates a transitive dependency.

3NF version:

STUDENTS

Student_ID | Student_Name | Department_ID
-----------|--------------|--------------
1          | Rahim        | 10
2          | Karim        | 20

DEPARTMENTS

Department_ID | Department_Name
--------------|----------------
10            | CSE
20            | EEE
